    What to Do Immediately After a Car Accident in Dallas

    At the Accident Scene

    1. Ensure safety first – Move to a safe area if possible and turn on hazard lights.
    2. Call 911 – Report the accident and request medical assistance if anyone is injured.
    3. Document everything – Take photos of vehicles, injuries, and road conditions.
    4. Gather information – Exchange driver’s license, insurance, and contact details.
    5. Do not admit fault – Stick to the facts when speaking with police.
    6. Seek medical attention – Even if you feel “fine” at the scene.

    Within 24 Hours

    1. Call our Dallas clinic for a same-day injury evaluation.
    2. Contact a personal injury attorney to protect your legal rights.
    3. Notify your insurance company and report the accident.
    4. Keep detailed records of symptoms, missed work, and expenses.
    5. Follow through with treatment – do not delay medically-necessary care.

    Many serious auto accident injuries have delayed symptoms. Adrenaline and shock can mask pain for hours or days. Early medical evaluation is crucial for both your health and your legal case.

    Letter of Protection (LOP) for Motor Vehicle Accident Cases

    A Letter of Protection (LOP) is a legal agreement between your personal injury attorney and our medical practice that guarantees payment for your treatment from your future settlement. This allows you to receive the motor vehicle accident injury care you need without worrying about immediate medical bills.

    What a Letter of Protection Means for You

    • ✅ No upfront payment required
    • ✅ Immediate access to comprehensive care
    • ✅ Treatment continues throughout your legal case
    • ✅ Medical bills paid directly from settlement funds

    How Our LOP Process Works

    1. Your attorney sends us a Letter of Protection.
    2. We schedule a same-day or next-day appointment.
    3. Comprehensive evaluation and ongoing treatment begin.
    4. We provide detailed medical documentation for your case.
    5. Payment is made from your settlement once your case resolves.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Motor Vehicle Accident Care

    How soon should I see a doctor after a car accident?

    You should see a doctor immediately, even if you feel fine. Many serious injuries from motor vehicle accidents have delayed symptoms, and early documentation is crucial for both your health and legal case.

    What if I don't have health insurance?

    We accept Letters of Protection (LOP) from personal injury attorneys, allowing you to receive treatment with no upfront payment. We also offer flexible payment plans for uninsured patients.

    Will my treatment be covered if the other driver was at fault?

    In most cases, yes. The at-fault driver's liability insurance may be responsible for your medical expenses. We can coordinate with your attorney and accept a Letter of Protection while your case is pending.

    How long will my treatment take?

    Treatment length depends on the type and severity of your injuries and how your body responds to care. After your initial evaluation, we’ll create a personalized treatment plan and estimated timeline.

    Can you help me find a personal injury attorney?

    Yes. We maintain relationships with experienced Dallas personal injury attorneys and can provide referrals to legal partners who regularly handle motor vehicle accident cases.

    Do you provide expert testimony for legal cases?

    Yes. Dr. Patel can provide expert medical testimony, review medical records, and offer professional opinions regarding your injuries and treatment related to the motor vehicle accident.

    What if my accident happened while I was working?

    Work-related vehicle accidents may be covered under workers compensation. Our clinic handles both workers’ comp and personal injury cases and can help you navigate your options.
